Privacy Policy

Conference CoPilot · effective 12 August 2026

What we store. Without an account, everything you enter stays in your own browser (localStorage) — nothing is sent anywhere. If you sign in, your pipeline and researcher profile are stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database in the ap-south-1 (Mumbai) region, protected by row-level security so only your authenticated session can read or write your rows.

What we collect. Your email address (for magic-link sign-in) and the research-planning data you choose to enter: venues, statuses, notes, drafts, and profile text. No analytics, no trackers, no cookies beyond the authentication session.

Share links. If you create a read-only share link, anyone holding that link can view your pipeline and profile (never your email or account details) until you revoke it. Treat links like passwords.

Third parties. Authentication emails are delivered by Supabase's mail service. The public CFP feed is built from publicly available conference listings and contains no personal data.

Your rights. Export everything at any time (Export JSON). To delete your account and all server-side data, email the maintainer below; deletion cascades through all stored rows.
