# Conference CoPilot — Beta Programme

A free tool for researchers to track conference calls-for-papers, deadlines,
and their submission pipeline — born inside a PhD on Self-Determination
Theory, now open to ~10 beta researchers from the µLearn / GTech community.

## What you get
- A deadline dashboard with drill-down and calendar export (with alarms)
- A submission pipeline (watching → submitted → accepted → presented) that
  syncs across your devices via magic-link sign-in
- A CFP feed refreshed twice weekly, with predatory-venue screening badges
- Abstract drafting per paper, submission-route detection, and read-only
  share links for your supervisor

## Quick start (3 minutes)
1. Open **https://deepusnath.github.io/conference-copilot/**
2. Answer the five setup questions — your pipeline arrives pre-matched to
   your papers' keywords (or explore the example workspace first)
3. Click **☁ Sign in to sync** to keep it on all your devices

## What we ask
- Use it for your *real* pipeline for at least two weeks
- File what you notice — bugs, confusions, wishes — via
  [beta feedback](https://github.com/deepusnath/conference-copilot/issues/new?template=beta-feedback.md)
  (or just WhatsApp Deepu)
- One 15-minute feedback chat at the end

## Privacy
Your data is yours: browser-local until you sign in, then row-level-secured
in Postgres. Export everything anytime. Details: [privacy policy](https://deepusnath.github.io/conference-copilot/privacy.html).
